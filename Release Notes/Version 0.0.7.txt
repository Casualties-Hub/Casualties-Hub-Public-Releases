Version 0.0.7

What changed
- Official public v0.0.7 release.
- Easter Eggs are now a compact toggle under Accessibility and are off by default.
- Existing settings migrate safely to the new default without changing game or mod files.
- Updated in-app version details, local release notes, and the public README.
- Refreshed the in-app CH Discord invite link.

Current features
- Nexus Dashboard, Local Mods, Protected Assets, Hub Center, settings, share codes, diagnostics, and optional online services remain available.
- Hub Online Services remain optional and can be turned off from Hub Center.

Known issue
- Opera GX may not automatically open or come to the foreground when Casualties Hub opens a browser link.
