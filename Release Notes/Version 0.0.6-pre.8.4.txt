Version 0.0.6-pre.8.4

What changed
- Added a first-launch Online Services choice with online services disabled by default.
- Players can explicitly enable announcements, maintenance status, and Hub update checks, or keep those services off.
- Nexus metadata browsing remains available as a separate required online feature when the player refreshes or browses metadata.
- Existing users keep their saved online-services preference and are not prompted again.

Known issue
- Opera GX may not automatically open or come to the foreground when Casualties Hub opens a browser link.
